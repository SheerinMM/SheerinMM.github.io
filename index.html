Things I've Built

AI · NLP · Full Stack2026
FakeGuard — Time-Aware Fake News Detection

A full-stack fake news detection platform that monitors articles continuously and updates credibility scores as stories evolve. Three parallel NLP engines: mutation tracking (spaCy, difflib), emotional manipulation scoring (VADER, TextBlob), and temporal credibility decay (NewsAPI). Fine-tuned RoBERTa and BERT on LIAR dataset (12,791 PolitiFact-labelled statements) on Tesla T4 GPU. Full analytics dashboard with score timeline showing credibility changes over time.

0.996 ROC-AUC
