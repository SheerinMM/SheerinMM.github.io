FakeGuard — Time-Aware Fake News Detection

End-to-end fake news detection platform combining transformer classification with three parallel NLP engines: content mutation analysis (spaCy, difflib), emotional manipulation scoring (VADER, TextBlob), and temporal credibility decay tracking (NewsAPI). Fine-tuned RoBERTa and BERT on the LIAR dataset (12,791 PolitiFact-labelled statements) on Tesla T4 GPU, with preprocessing, cross-validation, SHAP explainability and error-analysis workflows. FastAPI REST services with automated article monitoring and a live analytics dashboard.
